▸   About the Trilogy   ◂

The world you know is gone. In its place — a fractured Earth of warlords, ruins, and dwindling hope — a handful of survivors carry the weight of everything humanity once was, and everything it might still become.

At the heart of it all: an artificial intelligence so vast and so strange that it contains an entire world within it. A virtual realm built from a child's imagination, haunted by ghosts, governed by ancient myth, and quietly watched over by a being older than memory — known only as Marduk.

Through three novels, we follow soldiers and scientists, prophets and exiles, children who grow into legends and old men who finally understand what they were made for. Their stories unspool across two realities — the physical wasteland of a broken civilisation, and the shimmering, perilous interior of a mind that may be God, or something older.

The Ghosts of Men is an epic of survival and faith — a war between truth and the lie that has been fought since the first day, and must be fought to the last. It asks what we owe the dead, what we owe the living, and whether the universe was made with a purpose — or whether purpose is something only the brave dare to choose.
